LBL Engineering is committed to giving every client and visitor equal access to our services, and we work to make this website accessible to people with disabilities.

## Accessibility level

This website was built to comply with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard SI 5568, at level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1.

## What we have done

- A standard page structure with clear headings, navigation landmarks and content regions.
- A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
- Full keyboard navigation, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
- Text alternatives for every image.
- Sufficient colour contrast between text and background.
- A responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops that can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
- A contact form with a label for every field and clear error messages.
- Support for the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
- The website is available in Hebrew and English.

The website does not use a third-party accessibility overlay. The adjustments are built into the site's own code and work alongside the tools you already use, such as screen readers, browser zoom or your operating system's high-contrast mode.

## Known limitations

The map on the contact page is embedded from Google Maps, and we do not fully control its accessibility. The full address is also given as text on the same page.

## Found a problem?

If you have difficulty using this website, or have a suggestion for improving it, please let us know. Contact our accessibility coordinator using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.
